## 0. Why re-read now

RESEARCH is 23 days old and NOTE-165 is 18. In that interval the repo gained a Z3 contract
tier (027), four live-session assessments and two plans (028), an ABI major and a minor
(017, 021), a second tree adopting the architecture, and one production defect that NOTE-165
had described in advance. None of that was in view when the two documents were written, and
neither document has been touched since. This note asks what still holds, what does not, and
what the ranking should be today. It changes nothing in RESEARCH or NOTE-165: per 027's
convention, the originals stay as the record of what was believed, and corrections live here.

## 3. NOTE-165's candidates, and the one that bit

NOTE-165 §16 listed sixteen candidates in three tiers. None of the tier-1 constructors exists
under the names proposed (`policy_for_input_budget`, `raw_window_for_input_budget`,
`policy_with_undersized_window`); the only substrate that landed is `bounded_scripted_ports`
itself (`src/core/test/stub_step.ail:348`, tested in `scripted_ports.ail`), which was PR #166. I did not audit the DST
guidance documents for the written rules (independent oracle, live control, boundary
triples), so "not written" is not claimed for those — see §8.

The tier-3 candidate is the one that matters.

**NOTE-165 §2 said:** the resolved limit `0` covers an unknown model, a catalogue miss,
disabled enforcement, a malformed window and an undersized window; "resolution erases the
reason before downstream policy and DST observe it"; and proposed a reason-preserving shape,
`Unknown | Disabled | Bounded(input_budget) | InvalidWindow(raw, allowance)`. NOTE-165 §5
added that configuration resolution is an unobserved integration boundary. §16 ranked both
**priority 3**.

**028 NOTE-005 found, on 2026-09-01:** compaction silently and permanently disabled on the
default profile. The active model has no catalogue row; `catalog_context_limit_for`
(`context_usage.ail:88`) returns `value: 0` at lines 91 and 94; `effective_input_limit`
(`compaction.ail:43`) maps that to 0 again; and every consumer defines 0 as *disabled*, each
correctly by its own contract — `usage_percent_with_limit` (`compaction.ail:26`) and, per
NOTE-005, the structural and AI compactors' guards. Real traffic of 51K tokens rendered as
`usage_pct: 0`. NOTE-008 confirmed the same on 2026-09-03 at roughly 230K real tokens.
NOTE-005 calls it "the highest-consequence finding of the session", and of the consumers:
"no single one of them is wrong. The defect is compositional."

The representation today is unchanged from 18 August: `ContextLimitRead = { value: int, … }`
and `effective_input_limit: int -> int`. The reason is still erased at the first boundary.

Two readings of this, both recorded. The kind one: NOTE-165 was a lessons note, not a plan,
and the lesson was correct. The useful one: a "priority 3, core policy" label on a finding
with a named live failure mode is a ranking error, and the ranking error is why nothing
happened. §6 corrects it.

## 4. What changed the picture

### 4.1 A verification tier arrived that RESEARCH did not anticipate

027 shipped `make verify_core` with a solver-computed classification register. Today:

```
verify_core: 11 contracts proven, 0 unstated, 1 blocked; 0 files failed, 48 bare
verify_core: pinned classification -- 9 substantive, 2 tautology, 0 spec-equals-body, 1 unclassified
```

028 NOTE-002 then measured where the tier stops, and corrected the tree's own annotation:
the "SKIPPED — Z3 fragment requires non-higher-order" comments are **stale**. Folds auto-skip
because the callee-sort gate in `verify.go` runs before the HOF inliner, not because folds
are unencodable; W1–W3 there are a re-wiring, estimated at "a weekend". What remains a wall
after W1–W3 is closures in callee position and effectful bodies — probe 4 and the `IO`/`AI`
rows. `c2_loop` is 682 lines of both.

This is a second, independent argument for RESEARCH §2.D that RESEARCH did not have. §2.D
argued for `step : (StepState, WorldResponse) -> (StepState, [Command], [LedgerEvent])` on
sole emission, Row 7 decidability and multi-actor. Today the same shape is also the only
form of the loop the contract tier can ever reach, and the PSE research note
(`.agent/research/Probabilistic_Symbolic_Execution/`) says the same for symbolic execution:
`c2_loop` "is not symbolically executable today", and the §2.D refactor "is the enabler".

### 4.2 028 re-derived part of §2.B/§2.D without citing 013

028 NOTE-003 measured `session.ail` as a hub: 3,706 lines at its revision, nine exported
entry points, a 12-positional-parameter entry with adjacent same-typed ints, and `rpc.ail`
importing `src/core/test/stub_step` — a test fixture in the production import graph. At
`2192092` the entry-point count is eleven `run_v2*` functions in `session.ail` (seven
exported, `:2976–3518`) plus a five-function facade in `agent_loop_v2.ail`; the entry NOTE-003
named is `run_v2_with_scripted_ports` at `src/core/test/scripted_ports.ail:78`, not in
`session.ail`, and has 13 parameters at `2192092`, with `step_budget: int` and
`max_cost_millicents: int` separated by one `bool`; the `rpc.ail:17` import is as measured.

PLAN-002 item 3 splits `session.ail` into loop / DP7 gate / fixtures and moves `stub_step`
out of production; item 2 collapses the entry points to one plus an options record. Those
are step zero of RESEARCH §2.D's "incremental route: tool phase first", reached as
comprehension cost rather than DST reach. Neither document references the other: no file
under `028_*` mentions 013, and RESEARCH predates 028 by sixteen days. Amendment markers
pointing here were added to PLAN-002 items 2 and 3 alongside this note.

### 4.3 The ABI went 5.0 → 6.0 → 7.0, and §2.C's arithmetic changed

017's ADR-001 answered its Q1: the coverage unit is now `(extension, capability atom)`, not
`(extension, slot)`; there are 9 capability kinds; `profile_coverage_dst` enforces atom-level
rules (`atoms-covered-excluded-exhaustive`, `atom-not-registered`, N>1 under signed rules).
A registry generator exists (`tools/ext_registry_gen/generate.py`, gated by
`registry_gen_check`), and `declared_vs_performed` derives its subject list from it.

Two consequences for §2.C. The generator is the precondition §2.E named — "a generic runner
has to construct install sets programmatically anyway" — and it is built. But the validators
that RESEARCH open question 4 asked about have grown, not shrunk, and a generic runner has to
carry all of them: the question is answered in the expensive direction. The three drivers
also grew 12% in the interval, so the per-profile cost §2.C priced at ~890 lines is ~995
today.

### 4.4 §2.E now has an exit twin

RESEARCH §2.E: registration runs ambient before the driver dispatches anything. ABI 7.0 adds
the other end. `src/core/ext/exit_manifest.ail` renders every registered `ExitIntent` at the
end of each turn and publishes a manifest to a host-named path; the TUI reads that file at
process exit and executes it. The module header explains why it must be a file and not a
call: the runtime that holds the closures is a child that is normally gone by the time the
parent exits. So the *execution* of exit actions is outside the world and outside the
ledger by construction.

The live check (`4b37439`) is the concrete case. The render had been built with
`noop_ext_ports()`; the no-op `file_read` answered `present: false` to every path; every
manifest read `"actions": []`; and both suites stayed green because one injects a scripted
read and the other is handed hand-written manifests. That is §2.C's "zero routed effects ≠
zero effects" and §2.A2's per-adapter recording obligation in one incident, and the
2026-09-05 learning names the shape: a seam mocked from both sides is invisible to both
suites. The fix routes the render through `ext_ports_of(provider.ports, provider.world, …)`,
so the render is now world-mediated. The execution is not, and cannot be without moving it.

If §2.E is done it should be scoped as both ends. The `extension_registration_failure` fault
class RESEARCH wanted has a natural sibling, `exit_action_unexecutable`, and the same
absence: the catalogue has no lifecycle boundary at all.

### 4.5 A second tree adopted the architecture

`m-motoko-dst-refactor-migration.md` (sunholo, Planned 2026-08-11, P0) ratifies adopting
`main_dst`'s ports and DST framework. Its corrected reading of what DST reaches is, from
outside, exactly RESEARCH §2.C's:

> extension coverage is **1 of ~40 covered hooks substantively world-mediated**, across 15
> extensions; a whole profile's 32 covered hooks are *"entirely of no-ops"*. What the
> framework gives extensions is a **contract layer** … useless for "does fmt save tokens".

Two cautions before this is used as evidence for §2.C. Their "profiles" are runtime config
directories under `.motoko/config/`, resolved by `motoko_profile:` in an eval manifest — not
`ProfileDefinition`s — so a generic DST profile runner does nothing for the need that doc
states. And their Phase 1 gates on "the ABI settles", which it has not: the doc measured 5.0
and the tree is at 7.0. A second dependent tree raises the cost of §2.A and §2.D, since both
change `Ports`/`ExtPorts`. I could not confirm from this checkout whether #154 has merged;
`main_dst` is not an ancestor of `main` here.

### 4.6 011 moved without §2.A

RESEARCH §2.A claimed it was a prerequisite for 011 §3.2 (shrinking): "shrinking gets one
sequence to shrink instead of four cursors". 011's ADR-001 (2026-08-16) chose delta
debugging over the serialized `ExecutionProgram` with strict replay as the oracle, which
needs no change to the world representation. It is still Proposed and not implemented — no
`ddmin` in the tree — but the route does not run through §2.A.

011 ADR-002's WI-1 (closed 2026-08-17) bumped the program schema `execution-program/2 → /3`
and showed, by sweeping each commit separately, that no drawn value moved and no
`generator_version` bump was owed. That is a worked procedure for RESEARCH open question 2,
with a limit: it covers a schema change that adds a field. §2.A would change the interaction
vocabulary itself, which is the case the question actually asks about. The question stays
open; the procedure for answering it exists.

§2.A did gain two consumers RESEARCH did not list: 014 §3.3's replay-diff (`Φ_CH`) needs the
census total rather than 9-of-12, and the Cordis note's inverse-carrying commands need
commands-as-data. Net: one consumer lost, two gained, one dependent tree added as cost.

### 4.7 The paper is parked, not frozen

RESEARCH §2.D and §4 gate commands+interpreter on "after the paper freezes".
`papers/motoko-dst-report/DRAFT.md` was last committed 2026-08-11 ("1 review round"); its
header still reads FULL DRAFT with open `[VERIFY]` items to be settled "at freeze"; Linear
MOT-92 was closed the same day. Nothing has moved it in 25 days and nothing is scheduled to.
The gate is vacuous either way: declare the 11 August state frozen, or accept that "after the
paper freezes" is now.

1. **"§2.A is a prerequisite for shrinking."** Too strong — §4.6.
2. **"The DST sweep is the safety net; this is precisely the class of refactor the system was
   built to make safe."** True of the mechanism, and the gates fired correctly on every
   design error in the 7.0 branch (learning §7). But the premise assumes a green sweep. 011's
   closeout reports "the same seven reds as the `059dbd9` baseline" on 2026-08-17; 028
   NOTE-005 reports `make dst DST_JOBS=1` red on 3 targets in 789s on 2026-09-01;
   `DST_KNOWN_RED` is empty at `Makefile:493`. I did not run the sweep in this session. Before
   any item 4–7 work, the sweep's state at HEAD has to be established and the known-red list
   made truthful, or the net has holes nobody has mapped.
3. **"Sequence §2.D after the paper freezes."** Vacuous — §4.7.

## 7. Decision surface for an ADR

Stated as candidates. The ADR, if written, decides these; this note does not.

1. **Adopt items 1–3 as one cluster** with no production restructuring beyond the limit
   type. Rough cost: item 1 two to three days including the consumers and a DST arm for
   each resolution reason with a live control (NOTE-165 §8's three-arm shape); item 2 one
   day; item 3 an afternoon via the `ailang-feedback` channel. Each lands as a separate PR
   with its own gate.
2. **Merge §2.D into 028 PLAN-002 as a named phase after items 2–3 there**, owned by whichever
   project runs PLAN-002. 013 keeps the design record; 028 keeps the sequencing.
3. **Re-scope §2.E to registration and exit**, and add a `LifecycleBoundary` to the fault
   catalogue when either is driven.
4. **Establish the sweep's state before items 4–7** — §5.2 — and make `DST_KNOWN_RED` carry
   every standing red with its reason, as the Makefile comment already requires.
5. **Answer RESEARCH Q7:** items 1–3 go straight to a PLAN; §2.D goes to 028; §2.A needs the
   vocabulary spike RESEARCH already asked for, and now also a corpus-migration answer under
   the WI-1 procedure.
